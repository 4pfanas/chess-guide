Overview

The Goal

Chess is a two-player strategy game played on an 8×8 board. You control 16 pieces — White moves first. The aim is to checkmate your opponent's King: trap it so it cannot escape capture.

Core Rules

Key Rules

  • Check: Your King is under attack — you must immediately escape.
  • Checkmate: King in check with no escape = game over.
  • Stalemate: No legal moves but King is not in check = draw.
  • Capture: Land on an opponent's square to remove their piece.
  • Promotion: A Pawn reaching the far end becomes any piece (usually a Queen).
  • Castling: King moves two squares toward a Rook; Rook jumps to the other side. Only if neither piece has moved and the path is clear.
  • En passant: If a Pawn advances two squares past an enemy Pawn, that enemy Pawn may capture it as if it only moved one square.
Strategy

Beginner Tips

  • Control the center — pieces in the center have more power.
  • Develop your Knights and Bishops early.
  • Castle early to keep your King safe.
  • Don't move the same piece twice in the opening without good reason.
  • Think about your opponent's threats before making your move.
